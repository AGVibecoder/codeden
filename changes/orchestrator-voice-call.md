- **Voice calls with the orchestrator.** The phone button next to the microphone in the orchestrator's message box starts a hands-free conversation: the microphone stays on, what you say is sent each time you pause (even while the orchestrator works or speaks), and its answers are spoken with your read-aloud engine. Pick **Headset** to talk over an answer (a real word stops it; coughs and "uh" don't) or **Speakers** to have the microphone pause while it speaks. **Hang up** in the call panel ends it. Optionally start a call by saying a wake word (Settings → Voice → Dictation, "Hey Annie" by default), which listens with the Browser or Local (Whisper) engine only.
