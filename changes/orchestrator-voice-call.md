- **Voice calls with the orchestrator.** **Call** in the orchestrator's top bar starts a hands-free conversation: what you say is sent after a short pause, the orchestrator's answer is spoken with your read-aloud engine, then it listens again. **Hang up** in the call panel ends it. Optionally start a call by saying a wake word (Settings → Voice → Dictation, "Hey Annie" by default), which listens with the Browser or Local (Whisper) engine only.
