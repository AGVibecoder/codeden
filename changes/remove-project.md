- **Remove a project.** Right-click a project in the sidebar (or use its ⋯ button) and choose **Remove project**. After you confirm, its sessions stop and are archived, the project leaves CodeDen, and its folder is deleted from disk, permanently. The confirmation names the folder. CodeDen refuses to delete a drive root, your home folder, its own data folder, or a folder that holds another project or lies inside one.
