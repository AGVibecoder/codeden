- **Alerts when the orchestrator or a project manager replies.** When one of them finishes a turn, a toast shows who replied and the first line of the answer, with **Open**; while CodeDen is in the background you also get a desktop notification (with **System notifications** on). Workers never raise one, and there's no alert for the chat you're looking at or during a voice call with it. Turn it off under **Settings › Notifications › When the orchestrator or a project manager replies**.
