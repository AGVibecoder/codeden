- **Chatterbox read-aloud, on your own graphics card.** Pick **Chatterbox (local)** under Settings → Voice → Read aloud and press **Install**: CodeDen detects an NVIDIA or AMD Radeon card and installs its own Python, the matching PyTorch, Chatterbox and the model your language needs (Windows for now, about 10 GB). Choose one read-aloud language; English uses Chatterbox Turbo, other languages Chatterbox Multilingual. Annie is the built-in voice, and **Add voice** turns a 6 to 60 second speech clip into a voice of your own, played back straight away.
